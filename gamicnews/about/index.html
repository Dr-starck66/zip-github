About GAMIC News

GAMIC News is an independent technology publication focused on artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, mobile technology, consumer hardware, gaming and startups. Our goal is simple: explain what changed, why it matters and what readers should do with that information.

How we work

We prioritize primary sources, direct documentation and clearly attributed reporting. News coverage is separated from opinion. Automated systems may assist with research, transcription, classification and drafting, but published work is subject to editorial quality controls for sourcing, originality and accuracy.

Ownership

GAMIC News Media operates this publication. Commercial relationships do not determine editorial conclusions.
