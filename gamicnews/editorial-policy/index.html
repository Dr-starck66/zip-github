Editorial Policy

Accuracy, attribution and usefulness guide our coverage. We distinguish reported facts from analysis, link to original sources when practical and avoid presenting rumors as established facts.

Sources

Primary documentation, official statements, technical papers, regulatory filings and direct product information are preferred. Secondary reports are attributed and cross-checked when a claim is consequential or disputed.

Automation and AI

Automation can surface potential stories and assist editors, but it must preserve source URLs and cannot fabricate quotations, measurements or first-hand experience. Content that fails our source, originality or completeness checks is not published.

Conflicts and commerce

Affiliate or advertising relationships, when present, are disclosed. Advertisers do not receive approval rights over editorial coverage.
