Hotels in Düsseldorf: where should you stay?

Düsseldorf's hotel market is shaped by the old town, main station, trade fair and airport. Your main trip purpose should decide the district.

Best areas

Altstadt / Zentrum

Good for restaurants, shopping and leisure-focused stays.

Hauptbahnhof

Practical for rail-heavy trips and regional business travel.

Messe / Airport axis

Often the smartest choice for exhibitions or early flights.

How to compare

During major Messe Düsseldorf events, compare hotels along direct transport routes instead of searching only inside the centre. Include breakfast, cancellation, local transport and airport transfer in the total trip cost.
