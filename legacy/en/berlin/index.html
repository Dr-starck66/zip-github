Hotels in Berlin: where should you stay?

Berlin is too large for a single 'best area'. Mitte works for first-time sightseeing, Hauptbahnhof for rail-heavy trips, and areas such as Charlottenburg or Friedrichshain can be better for specific plans.

Best areas for different trips

Mitte

Best for many landmark-heavy first visits and short stays where walking matters.

Hauptbahnhof

Useful for early trains, late arrivals and regional day trips.

Charlottenburg / City West

A strong option for western Berlin, shopping and travellers who prefer a less monument-focused base.

How to compare hotels

Choose the transport line before the postcode. A direct U-Bahn or S-Bahn connection often matters more than a few kilometres on the map.

Compare the total stay rather than the headline room rate: cancellation terms, breakfast, parking, local transport and the time needed to reach your main destination can change the real value significantly.
