Hotels in Cologne: choosing the right area

FreeHotels.info historically focused on hotel reservations and hotel information in Germany and across Europe. This rebuilt Cologne page preserves that travel theme while giving travellers useful, current guidance on choosing an area to stay.

Where to stay in Cologne

The most useful areas to compare include Old Town, Hauptbahnhof, Deutz and the trade fair. A central postcode is not always the best choice: consider the journeys you will actually make, public transport and the distance to stations, attractions, venues or the airport.

Central areas

For a short city break, a central hotel can save time and make many sights walkable. Check real walking distances on a map and read recent information about street noise, room position and late-night access before booking.

Station and transport connections

Train travellers and one-night visitors may benefit from staying near the main station. A direct metro, tram or suburban-rail connection can also make a less central hotel more practical and better value.

Compare the total cost

Look beyond the headline room rate. Breakfast, parking, local taxes, cancellation terms and payment conditions all affect the real cost. During fairs, concerts and major events, flexible dates and alternative well-connected districts can produce much better value.
