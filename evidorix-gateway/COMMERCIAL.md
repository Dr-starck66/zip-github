# Evidorix — Commercial Positioning

Category: Evidence Control Plane for AI and agent reliability.

Core proposition: Evidorix does not ask whether an AI sounds confident. It asks whether each important claim survives evidence verification, counter-testing and reproducibility gates.

Beachhead customers: agentic AI engineering teams, DevOps/platform teams, AI QA/eval teams, regulated SaaS teams and automation agencies.

Packaging hypothesis: Local free; Team target 9,900 EUR/year after production auth/persistence hardening; Enterprise custom pricing. These are pricing hypotheses, not existing revenue.

A 50,000 EUR asking price for the software asset can be framed around source code, brand, methodology, benchmark suite, API/connector architecture, transfer documentation, roadmap and optional exclusivity/support. A 50,000 EUR market valuation is not proven without external commercial evidence such as a paid pilot, LOI, buyer offer, usage or a third-party benchmark.
