Compact crossover: 2027 Hyundai Kona

The Kona SE FWD was listed at $239 per month for 36 months with $3,999 at signing and 10,000 miles per year. It has a low headline payment, but an effective monthly estimate of approximately $343 using our transparent assumption. Confirm whether incentives are available in your region and whether the specific SE FWD trim is offered by the local dealership.

Rugged SUV: Wrangler Sport S

The Wrangler's selected deal runs 42 months. That matters: compared with a standard 36-month lease, it obligates the lessee for another six months. The comparison should incorporate those extra installments, annual mileage limits and possible early-termination exposure. A 42-month arrangement is not automatically worse, but a shopper should knowingly choose the longer term.

Three-row family option: Kia Telluride

The 2027 Telluride S FWD was reported at $429 per month over 36 months, with $3,999 due at signing. If a family needs more passenger or cargo space, that utility may be worth the higher payment. It is important to compare the exact trim and avoid treating an upper-trim photo as proof of features included in the promotional S model.

Luxury alternative: Genesis GV80

The selected GV80 2.5T Select AWD lease used a 24-month term and $5,999 due upfront. A shorter lease can preserve flexibility, yet high upfront cash carries risk if the vehicle is stolen or totaled. Examine the complete lease contract, insurance and gap coverage, and conditions for any up-front reduction.

What to check before selecting an SUV lease

Ask whether four-wheel or all-wheel drive is included, confirm charging or fuel considerations for hybrids, match mileage to your actual driving, and compare insurance quotations for the exact trim. Use our effective payment calculator and upfront cash guide before visiting the dealership.
