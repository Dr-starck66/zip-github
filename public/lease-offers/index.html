Real-world comparison: two SUVs with the same headline payment

An October report listed a 2027 Hyundai Kona at $239/month with $3,999 at signing, and a 2026 Corolla sedan at $239/month with $2,999 at signing. Both used 36-month terms. Their published monthly amounts match, but the extra $1,000 at signing makes the Kona's illustrative monthly equivalent about $28 higher. A decision must also consider that one is a small SUV and the other a sedan, plus region, taxes and credit eligibility.

What an archived publisher match does—and does not—prove

Ten archived price records now match the displayed August publisher data; the other eight visible figures are provisional October reports. This is evidence of historical publication, not current availability directly from a manufacturer or dealer. For a current quote you still need a manufacturer deal page matching your ZIP code, a named trim and exact mileage, a complete first-payment breakdown, and current expiry/eligibility information. Only then can the active-offer status be upgraded.

How the automatic verification gate should work

  1. Retrieve and timestamp the original publication and the manufacturer promotion page, respecting site rules and reasonable request limits.
  2. Match make, year, trim, term, advertised payment, signing cash, mileage, ZIP applicability and offer expiry; decline ambiguous matches.
  3. Mark entries as confirmed only when all mandatory fields match, otherwise keep the offer as reported-but-unverified.
  4. Recompute effective cost, create an audit receipt, inspect the published HTML and update sitemap only when the production domain is active.
  5. Retire expired promotions without inventing replacements. Keep the comparison history labeled as historical, for transparency.

Limitations before a binding lease decision

The 18 visible historical price examples omit your particular tax, title, registration, dealer fees, insurance, early-termination charges, mileage overages, wear-and-tear expenses and possible acquisition/disposition charges unless an exact source spells them out. We do not submit credit applications or broker financing. Use the calculator with the complete dealer worksheet and read every term before signing. Promotions may require special credit tiers, conquest/loyalty incentives or limited-stock vehicles.
